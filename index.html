Tax depreciation register (capital allowances)

Capital allowances (Singapore): Computer Equipment and Software Development are written off 100% in the year they enter service (S19A one-year write-off); Furniture, Fixtures and Leasehold over 3 years. Allowances are not time-apportioned — a full annual allowance is claimed in each year of assessment the asset is in use. WIP claims nothing until it transfers in. The Deferred tax column shows the liability on the temporary difference (accounting NBV − tax WDV) at your set rate.

Finalise / lock

Locks the current entity's year so no edits, imports, deletions or settings changes can be made — for a finalised register. Applies per entity (AUS155 / AUS501 each lock independently). You can still view, export and switch entities. Unlock here to make changes.

Sample & reset

Switch entities from the Entity selector in the header — each keeps its own register, settings and reporting date. AUS155 is the Singapore register (30 Jun 2026). AUS501 is the CB Financial Services (UK) register for FY25 (1 Jul 2024 – 30 Jun 2025); accounting depreciation is computed on a day-count basis. UK capital allowances (pooled) are a follow-up — the tax register is a placeholder for AUS501.
