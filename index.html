Fixed Asset Register

Fixed Asset Register Outline

This page summarizes a practical structure for an asset register covering both accounting and tax requirements.

1. Asset Master Information

  • Asset ID / tag number
  • Description and category
  • Location, department, and custodian
  • Acquisition date and useful life

2. Acquisition Details

  • Supplier and invoice reference
  • Purchase cost and installation cost
  • Customs, duties, and capitalized costs

3. Accounting Register

  • Opening carrying amount
  • Additions and disposals
  • Depreciation and accumulated depreciation
  • Closing carrying amount

4. Tax Register

  • Tax base cost
  • Capital allowances and depreciation
  • Balancing adjustments on disposal
  • Closing tax written-down value

5. Controls and Reconciliation

  • Physical verification
  • Supporting documents
  • Reconciliation to the general ledger
  • Audit trail and approvals
