Accounting depreciation register

Movement by asset category for the selected financial year: opening carrying amount, additions, transfers (WIP placed in service), depreciation charge, disposals and closing net book value. Click a category to expand its individual assets; expand an asset for its full year-by-year schedule.

Tax depreciation register (capital allowances)

Tax written-down values using capital-allowance rates (diminishing value or prime cost), independent of the accounting register. Transfers show WIP items placed in service during the year. Click a category to expand its individual assets.
